    Export the CSV from your Public Force Attendance app (check-in/check-out tool), then upload it here.     How the duty roster works

    1. Say how many guards each site needs. In Settings, under Sites, enter the guards required on the day shift and the night shift.
    2. Pick a month and a site. You see every active employee assigned to that site, one row each, with a box for every day.
    3. Click a day's box to plan it. It cycles through D (day shift), N (night shift), OFF (weekly off) and blank (not planned). Each click saves straight away.
    4. Read the coverage check under the roster. For every day it counts who is on day and night shift against what the site needs, and flags Short-staffed where you are under.

    The roster is a plan. Attendance, which records who actually turned up, is separate. Changing the roster does not change attendance, payroll or invoices.

    Duty roster

    Plan who's on duty before the day happens — separate from Attendance, which records who actually showed up. Click a cell to cycle: Unscheduled → Day shift → Night shift → Off → Unscheduled.

    Payroll settings

    These defaults drive every calculation. Edit them to match current rules — they are a starting point, not verified legal figures.

    ESI in 2026: the wage limit is still ₹21,000 a month (₹25,000 for a person with a disability), with 0.75% from the employee and 3.25% from the employer. The 17 September 2026 change was for PF only, and a higher ESI limit has been discussed but not notified.

    Provident fund (PF)

    How PF is worked out in payroll and what is paid to the EPFO each month. The standard figures are filled in. Change them only if the rules change, and confirm with your CA.

    New wage ceiling. The PF wage ceiling went from ₹15,000 to ₹25,000 from 17 September 2026 (notification S.O. 5109(E)). Payroll uses the ceiling in force on each day, so September 2026 is split: 1 to 16 September on ₹15,000 and 17 to 30 September on ₹25,000. Staff with Basic + D.A. up to ₹25,000 now have to be covered by PF.
    Shares
    PF wage ceiling
    PF wages are Basic + D.A. For staff on the pay structure that is their Basic + D.A.; for staff paid a single wage it is the "Basic % of gross" in Payroll settings above. Above the ceiling, PF is on the ceiling unless the employee is marked "PF on full Basic" in the employee form. Pension and insurance are always on wages up to the ceiling.

    Payslip signature and approval stamp

    Once a payroll is approved, every payslip downloaded from it carries the signature image below and an "Electronically approved" stamp showing who approved it, when, and a reference. Nothing needs signing by hand.

    Sign with dark ink on plain white paper and take a photo or scan. The paper is removed and the picture is cropped automatically.

    Invoice letterhead and bank details

    Printed on every invoice PDF below the totals. The letterhead (crest, address, GSTIN, PAN, Udyam, EPF and ESIC numbers) is built in. Leave a field blank to leave it off the invoice.

    998525 is guard services. Ask your CA if you also supply other services.

    Company profile (brochure)

    A 5-page company profile PDF with your registrations and contact details. You can also attach it when you email a rate card from the phone app. Anything you leave empty is left out of the PDF. Please read every line before you send it to a client.

    Backup

    Download a copy of everything in the system and keep it somewhere safe and private, such as your own computer or a private drive. It holds personal details like Aadhaar numbers and bank accounts, so never send it on WhatsApp or by email.

    The data backup holds employees, attendance, payroll, invoices, clients, contracts, rate cards, licences, settings and the email log. The ZIP holds the photos and documents uploaded in the employee forms. Make both at least once a month. The dashboard reminds you when a month has passed.

    Restore from a backup file

    Use this if records were deleted or changed by mistake. Choose a data backup file (the .json), check it, and tick what to bring back. Nothing is deleted: a record in the file replaces the same record in the system, and records that are only in the system stay as they are. Your present data is downloaded first as a safety copy. Staff logins and uploaded files are not part of this: the files stay in storage and the logins stay in Supabase.

    Payroll groups

    Run payroll separately for a client or site, and for a role. Each group has its own attendance, calculation, approval and payslips, and its own pay day. Everyone who is not in a group stays in "Everyone else", which is the normal payroll. An employee goes into the most specific group that matches: site and role together beat a site alone, and a site alone beats a role alone.

    New group

    The site, role and first month of a group cannot be changed once it is made. To change them, end the group after its last month and make a new one. A group can be deleted only while it has no payroll. The pay day is the day of the next month by which the salary should be paid.

    Additional pay

    PF is not worked out on these lines. Leave the box ticked for regular pay such as incentives and allowances, and unticked for a bonus or a reimbursement.

    Recalculate the payroll after adding pay. To take money off, use "Other deduction" instead.

    Change pay

    Pay history

    Pay is worked out day by day, so a change in the middle of a month is shared out by days. A rise can go back to an earlier date and is paid as arrears. A cut can only start in a month whose payroll is not yet approved, needs the employee's agreement, and can never go below the minimum wage. Download the letter from the history and give it to the employee.

    Change pay for many employees

    Raise or lower the pay of a site or a role from one date, for example an annual increment or a minimum wage revision. Check the list before you apply it.

    Amounts are per month. For daily-wage staff the amount is per day. For staff on the pay structure, a percentage changes Basic and D.A. together and an amount is added to or taken from Basic.
