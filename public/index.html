    Import from the Attendance app

    Export the CSV from your Public Force Attendance app (check-in/check-out tool), then upload it here. Matching is done by Employee ID — any day an employee has a check-in record is marked Present; any day without one (for that employee, within the CSV's date range) is marked Absent, unless you've already set it to Half-day or Paid Leave here.

    How the duty roster works

    1. Say how many guards each site needs. In Settings, under Sites, enter the guards required on the day shift and the night shift.
    2. Pick a month and a site. You see every active employee assigned to that site, one row each, with a box for every day.
    3. Click a day's box to plan it. It cycles through D (day shift), N (night shift), OFF (weekly off) and blank (not planned). Each click saves straight away.
    4. Read the coverage check under the roster. For every day it counts who is on day and night shift against what the site needs, and flags Short-staffed where you are under.

    The roster is a plan. Attendance, which records who actually turned up, is separate. Changing the roster does not change attendance, payroll or invoices.

    Duty roster

    Plan who's on duty before the day happens — separate from Attendance, which records who actually showed up. Click a cell to cycle: Unscheduled → Day shift → Night shift → Off → Unscheduled.

    Add employees to this site first, or choose a different site.

    Coverage check

    Set how many guards you need per shift for this site in Settings → Sites, and this will flag any day you're short.

    Payroll settings

    These defaults drive every calculation. Edit them to match current rules — they are a starting point, not verified legal figures.

    Professional Tax slabs

    Monthly PT slabs by state. Tamil Nadu and Kerala assess PT half-yearly through municipal bodies rather than a simple monthly salary slab — leave their slabs at ₹0 and set PT manually per employee if needed.

    Rate card defaults

    Default statutory % and fixed amounts used whenever you create a new client rate card. Matches your standard wage break-up sheet.

    Invoice letterhead and bank details

    Printed on every invoice PDF below the totals. The letterhead (crest, address, GSTIN, PAN, Udyam, EPF and ESIC numbers) is built in. Leave a field blank to leave it off the invoice.
